CES · Customer Exception System

Payment Exception Pipeline from unordered queue to assisted decision

Unidentified insurance payments used to reach an analyst as a flat, unranked list with no supporting evidence — each case reconstructed by hand from customer, policy and payment-history records. This pipeline computes 19 signals per payment, classifies it into one of 5 exception scenarios, and delivers a ranked queue where every case arrives with its reasoning attached. The rule engine owns every decision; the LLM only explains it.
